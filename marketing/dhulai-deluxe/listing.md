## Full description
An auto-rickshaw that hasn't seen water since monsoon. An elephant statue wearing a decade of dust. A water tanki that's basically archaeology. One power jet. Your thumb. Mud melts into color wherever you linger, every finished panel pops a sparkle, and the clean-meter climbs toward that one perfect number.

HOW TO PLAY
• Hold and drag — the water jet follows your thumb
• Panels bloom from mud-brown to full color
• The object slowly rotates so you miss nothing
• 100% = CHAMAK GAYA ✨ → next filthy thing

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Deep-clean ASMR without the water bill
• Three rotating objects, endless dirt
• One-thumb, fully offline
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the foam-blast ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. Dirtier things coming.
