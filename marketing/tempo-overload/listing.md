## App name
Tempo Overload

## Short description (77/80)
The stack is too tall. The road disagrees. Deliver whatever physics forgives.

## Full description
Someone loaded the tempo with a tower of boxes taller than the driver's optimism, and the delivery gate is at the bottom of a pothole farm. Every swerve ripples up the stack — top boxes loosest — and anything that sways too far cartwheels into the scenery.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag to steer (gently; the stack remembers everything)
• Potholes kick the stack — swerve or suffer
• Deliver through the arch: pay per surviving box
• Next trip: taller stack, meaner road

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Sway physics you steer with your stomach
• Boxes tumble off with full cartoon dignity
• One-thumb drag, fully offline
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the cargo-straps ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. Ghat roads coming.
