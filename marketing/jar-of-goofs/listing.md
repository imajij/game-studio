## App name

## Short description (71/80)
Drop, merge and discover 11 goofy creatures. How big can your goof get?

## Full description
Drop goofy creatures into the jar. When two of the same kind touch, they merge into an even goofier one — from tiny Bloop all the way up to the legendary Cosmic Bloop.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap to drop a creature into the jar
• Match two of the same kind to merge them
• Keep the pile under the danger line
• Fill your Goofdex by discovering all 11 creatures

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• One-thumb, pick-up-and-play rounds
• 11 original hand-drawn sticker creatures, each with its own silly bio
• Satisfying squishy merges with pops, particles and screen shake
• Works fully offline — no account, no sign-up

Made with love by a solo developer. Free to play, supported by family-safe ads. An optional ad can shake the jar when you're in trouble.
