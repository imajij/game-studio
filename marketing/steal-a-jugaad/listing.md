## Short description (78/80)

## Full description
The bazaar conveyor is rolling and the weirdest creatures in town are walking by with price tags: Samosa Sherni, Chai Walrus, Auto Ostrich, Disco Buffalo. Grab them before they walk off, park them in your godown, and watch the rupees roll in — even while you're away.

Feeling greedy? RAID the rival godown. Its guard is asleep… mostly. Sneak when his eye is shut, FREEZE when it opens, and walk out with something shiny. And keep your own stock safe — a tapping-fast thief shows up when you least expect it.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap conveyor creatures to buy them before they leave
• Every creature earns ₹ per second on its pedestal
• RAID: hold to sneak, release to freeze — red eye means FREEZE
• Thief alert: tap him fast or lose your newest creature
• HOT DROP rotates every 10 minutes — 2× spawn rate on one creature

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• 10 original creatures across 5 rarities, Common to Mythic
• Offline earnings while you're away (4 hour cap)
• One-thumb controls, fully offline — raids are vs the game's AI, no account
• Free to play, supported by family-safe ads — rewarded ads are always optional

Early access: a brand-new game still growing new creatures, zones and defenses. Made with love by a solo developer.
