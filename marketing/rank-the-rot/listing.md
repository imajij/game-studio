## Short description (77/80)

## Full description
Gyaan Pigeon. WiFi Chacha. Tubelight Tiger. Golgappa Ghost. You know EXACTLY which of these is S-tier — but does the panel agree?

Flick each creature card into S, A, B or F. When your list is done, the daily Mohalla Panel verdict is revealed and your taste gets scored. Nail it and you're CERTIFIED TASTE. Miss badly and… the panel is disappointed. The panel verdict reshuffles every single day — come back tomorrow and argue again.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe up = S, right = A, down = B, left = F
• 8 cards per deck from a cast of 10 original creatures
• Exact tier match = 3 points, one tier off = 1
• New panel verdict every calendar day

(The "panel" is the game's own daily jury — same verdict for every player that day. Fully offline, no voting servers, no accounts.)

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• 45-second rounds, instant redeal
• That "HOW is Thermocol Titan an F?!" feeling, daily
• One-thumb, fully offline
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the peek ad is always optional

Early access: a brand-new game from a solo developer. More creatures coming.
