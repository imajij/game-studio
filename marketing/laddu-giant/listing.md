## App name
Laddu Giant

## Short description (69/80)
Every laddu makes you bigger. The wall at the end finds out how much.

## Full description
A street paved with laddus and one rule: eat. Each one makes you bigger, rounder, faster-shadowed. Rocks shrink you. Squeeze-gates trim you down to size (with sparkles, out of respect). And at the end — a 280-brick candy wall that crumbles exactly as deep as you've grown.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag to steer, eating is automatic
• Laddus +size, rocks −size, gates cap your size
• The final smash converts size to coins, brick by flying brick
• Longer, rockier galis every level

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• The purest power fantasy: get big, break wall
• The camera pulling back as you outgrow the street
• One-thumb drag, fully offline
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the ×2-start ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. Mega-walls coming.
