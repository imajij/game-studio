## Short description (65/80)

## Full description
The crane swings. The freaks wobble. One tap decides everything.

Drop square little weirdos — Blobby Greg, Mister Loaf, Angry Flan and friends — onto the tower and see how high you can go before physics betrays you.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap to release the freak from the swinging crane
• Land them square on the tower — perfect drops earn bonus points
• Three misses and the tower's done

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Pure one-thumb timing fun
• Real wobbly physics — every tower leans differently
• 6 original hand-drawn freaks
• Quick rounds, instant restart, works offline

Free to play, supported by family-safe ads. Stuck? An optional ad glues your tower solid.
