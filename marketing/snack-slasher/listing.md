## Full description
Snacks are flying. Your finger is a blade. Slice everything… except Sir Soap.

Swipe through Pizza Pete, Donut Diane, Burrito Bandit and the rest of the snack gang as they tumble across the kitchen. Chain multiple snacks in one stroke for combo points — but one careless swipe through Sir Soap and it's all over. Nobody wants soap for dinner.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe to slice snacks mid-air
• Slice 3+ in one stroke for combos
• Let a snack drop and you lose a life
• NEVER slice Sir Soap

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Fast, juicy swipe action
• 6 original hand-drawn snack characters
• Combo chains, slice trails and crunchy effects
• Works fully offline

Free to play, supported by family-safe ads. An optional ad gets you back in with full lives.
