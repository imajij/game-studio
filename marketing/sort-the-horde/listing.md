## Full description
The dungeon daycare has a mix-up problem: ghouls and babies keep arriving down the same chute, and SOMEONE has to sort them.

Swipe ghouls into the dungeon (left) and babies into the daycare (right). Easy — until the horde starts wearing disguises. That baby might have fangs. That ghoul might be wearing a very convincing bow.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe left for ghouls, right for babies
• Watch for disguises — look closely!
• Wrong sorts (and missed drops) cost lives
• Build streaks for bonus points

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Snappy swipe-sorting with a twist
• 6 original hand-drawn creatures + sneaky disguises
• Gets deliciously tricky as you go
• Works fully offline

Free to play, supported by family-safe ads. One optional ad un-fires you from the daycare.
