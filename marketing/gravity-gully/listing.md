## Short description (66/80)
Run the floor. Flip to the ceiling. The canyon only gets faster.

## Full description
A candy canyon with two floors — the floor, and the ceiling. Swipe up and gravity politely swaps sides: blocks you couldn't dodge become blocks you run OVER, upside down, hair-first. Subway-style sprinting with one extra dimension of panic.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe left/right to change lanes
• Swipe UP to flip gravity — floor ↔ ceiling
• Candy blocks live on BOTH surfaces; thread them
• Coins bank forever; speed climbs forever

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• The flip is instant, juicy and slightly heroic every time
• Sherbet-sunset canyon with Flow-painted candy walls
• One-thumb swipes, fully offline
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the revive ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. New canyons coming.
