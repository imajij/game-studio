## App name
Tile Toofan

## Short description (74/80)
Grab tiles, let the sea eat them one plank at a time, land on the ×5 zone.

## Full description
Island to island over a cartoon sea, and the only bridge is the one you're carrying. Every rainbow tile you run over hops onto your back; every step over water quietly takes one back. Reach the end with tiles to spare and the multiplier runway turns them into cold hard coins.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag to steer across islands
• Tiles stack on your back (watch the pile sway)
• Water lays planks from your stack automatically — empty stack = SPLASH
• Finish slide: leftover tiles = distance = ×1…×5 payout

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Risk math you can feel: fat stack vs short route
• Flow-painted turquoise waves all the way down
• One-thumb drag, fully offline
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the golden-tiles ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. Storm courses coming.
