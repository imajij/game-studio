## Short description (72/80)

## Full description
A pitch-dark neon arena. Robo-blobs hiding everywhere. Your torch cone is the only truth: whatever it touches is revealed, and one ZAP tags them out (they sit down, dramatically). But their cones work too — stand in one half a second too long and YOU'RE tagged.

HOW TO PLAY
• Joystick to sneak, your facing aims the torch cone
• ZAP tags the closest lit bot
• Three hearts; shielded moments after each hit
• Clear every bot → next round adds one more, faster

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Fog-of-war hide-and-seek — tension without a single weapon
• Neon-on-indigo style with candy walls
• One-thumb + one button, fully offline vs AI
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the heart-refill ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. More arenas coming.
