## Full description
Glass tubes, candy balls, one tidy obsession: one color per tube. Tap a tube and its top ball lifts hopefully; tap another and the whole matching run bounce-pours across. Wrong color? The tube politely refuses. Your undo button judges you silently.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap source, tap target — matching runs pour together
• Two spare tubes are your working space
• 3 colors grows to 7 as the vats climb
• Every level reverse-shuffled from solved = always solvable

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• The genre's cleanest dopamine, now with bouncy 3D pours
• Blush-pink room, wooden shelf, shimmering glass
• One-thumb taps, fully offline
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the undo-pack ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. Mystery tubes coming.
