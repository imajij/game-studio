## Short description (76/80)
The beat counts to five. YOU are six and seven. How long can you stay on it?

## Full description
One… two… three… four… five… — now TAP. The beat handles 1 to 5 by itself. Six and seven are yours, and they need to be EXACT. Land the pair and the beat speeds up. Again. And again.

HOW TO PLAY
• Watch the ring fill — beats 1–5 tick automatically
• Tap dead on "six", tap dead on "seven"
• PERFECT window = 2 points, good = 1, miss = a life
• Tapping early during 1–5 also costs a life (no mashing!)
• Three lives. Tempo ramps every pair.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• 10-second rounds — instant restart, zero menus
• Daily best resets every day; all-time best stays forever
• One-thumb, works fully offline, no sign-up
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the revive ad is always optional

Early access: a brand-new game from a solo developer. More modes coming.
