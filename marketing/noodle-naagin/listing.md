## App name
Noodle Naagin

## Short description (64/80)
Eat diyas, grow long, make the other noodles pop first.

## Full description
Night arena, glowing rangoli floor, five neon noodles and a floor full of diyas. Eat to grow. Grow to block. Block so the OTHER noodles pop — because any head that touches a body bursts into diyas for everyone else to loot. Including yours.

HOW TO PLAY
• Joystick sets your heading — the noodle never stops
• Diyas = +1 segment, rising pitch as you lengthen
• Walls and bodies pop you; cut rivals off so they pop first
• Popped noodles scatter their whole length as diyas

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Classic io-snake tension, fully offline vs AI
• Neon trails on a Flow-painted rangoli floor
• One-thumb, no account, no network
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the shield-revive ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. New arenas coming.
