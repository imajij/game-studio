# Mini Mandi — store listing

## App name
Mini Mandi

## Short description (79/80)
Pick, stack, sell, upgrade. Your little mandi runs on your own two thumbs. One.

## Full description
A sunny plot, a veggie patch, a stall with a pink canopy, and customers who pay in shiny coins. Walk over veggies and they leap onto your head-stack (it wobbles — that's the law), dump them at the stall, watch the queue light up. Stand on glowing pads to buy more patches, a bigger basket, faster chappals.

HOW TO PLAY
• Joystick to walk — picking, dropping and selling are automatic
• Stand on a pad ~1 second to buy its upgrade
• Customers take 1–3 veggies and pay on the spot
• Four upgrade pads, three patches, endless pottering

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Arcade-idle satisfaction: carry, dump, ka-ching
• The wobbling veggie stack is 90% of the charm
• One-thumb, fully offline, no account
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the ×2-pay ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. Helpers and new stalls coming.
