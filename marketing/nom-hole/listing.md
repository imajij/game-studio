# Nom Hole — store listing

## App name
Nom Hole

## Short description (73/80)
You are a hole. The town is lunch. Out-eat two rival holes in 90 seconds.

## Full description
Somewhere in a cheerful toy town, the ground opened and it was HUNGRY. That's you. Start cone-sized, swallow cones; grow crate-sized, swallow crates; end the round inhaling entire buses while two rival holes race you for every snack.

HOW TO PLAY
• Joystick the hole around town
• Anything smaller than you sinks in — gulp pitch drops as you grow
• 90 seconds, most points wins
• Everything is an empty toy prop — nobody is inside anything

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• The power curve: nibbling cones → inhaling buses
• The camera pulls back as you become the town's biggest problem
• One-thumb, fully offline vs AI holes
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the +30 s ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. Bigger towns coming.
