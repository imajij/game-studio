## Full description
Welcome to the only lab where "what if a jalebi and a tubelight had a baby" is a valid research question. Pick two essences, slam the FUSE button, and meet your creation — auto-named, half-tinted from each parent, and ready to fight.

Your Jalelight (POWER 3 · SPEED 5 · CHAOS 8) battles the Lab's own fusions in quick stat duels. Win trophies, build streaks, and unlock the two RARE essences — Dhol and Monsoon Cloud — for the strongest fusions in the lab.

HOW TO PLAY
• Pick 2 of 8 essences and FUSE
• Fusion stats = both parents added together
• Battles are 3-heart stat duels with a little luck on top
• Unlock both rare essences once, keep them forever

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• 28 possible fusions, every one auto-named (Cookoon. Pigelight. Monsbi.)
• 30-second battles, instant refuse… re-fuse
• One-thumb, fully offline — every opponent is the game's AI, no account
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the unlock ad is always optional

Early access: a brand-new game from a solo developer. More essences coming.
