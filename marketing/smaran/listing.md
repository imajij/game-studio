## Full description
Smaran (स्मरण — "remembrance") is a calendar built for families and elderly users: big readable text, obvious buttons, gentle reminders — and absolutely no account, internet or confusion.

WHY FAMILIES LOVE IT
• Big monthly calendar with coloured event dots
• Add an event in seconds: title, time, colour, optional photo and notes
• Reminders that actually remind — 30 minutes before, or your choice
• Agenda view: today, this week, this month as a simple list
• Large Text Mode and Dark Mode in settings
• Backup & restore your events to a file you control

100% OFFLINE
No sign-in. No sync servers. No data collection. Your family's schedule stays on your phone.

Perfect for: medicine schedules, doctor visits, birthdays, pooja days, school events, bill dates.

Built by a solo developer. Feedback shapes the roadmap.
