## Full description
One app for the whole health picture: log meals and calories, tick off water glasses, track sleep, plan workouts, and build habit streaks — all stored ONLY on your phone. No account. No cloud. No data leaves your device.

TRACK EVERYTHING
• Calories & macros — photo your plate or quick-add; daily goal from your own profile
• Water — one-tap glasses against a daily goal
• Sleep & workouts — simple logs that build a real picture
• Habits & streaks — daily/weekly habits with streak flames and a consistency chart

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything stays in a local database on your device
• Optional: plug in YOUR own free Google Gemini API key for AI food estimates — your key, your quota, used straight from your phone; works fine without it (local estimates)
• No sign-up, no ads account profiling, works fully offline

WHO IT'S FOR
Anyone who wants one honest dashboard for body and habits without surrendering their data to a server.

Built by a solo developer. Feedback shapes the roadmap.
