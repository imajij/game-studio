## Full description
The monsoon broke ALL records today and the flood wave is right behind you. Run. The street throws everything at you — and "everything" rotates: flood barrels and puddles, a full aunty stampede with flying chappals, and an entire wedding baraat, dhol drummers included.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe left/right to change lanes
• Swipe up to jump puddles, chappals and garlands
• Dodge the solid stuff — every hit lets the wave surge closer
• Run clean and the wave slowly falls back
• Grab umbrellas for bonus points

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Chase themes rotate every 400 m — FLOOD! AUNTY STAMPEDE! BARAAT INCOMING!
• The wave is always on screen — you can SEE how close doom is
• One-thumb, fully offline, no account
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the revive ad is always optional

Early access: a brand-new game from a solo developer. More chase themes coming.
