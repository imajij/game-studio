## Short description (77/80)

## Full description
Ten little blobs and a dream. Steer your mob down the festival track: green gates multiply it (×2! +25!), red walls thin it, and at the arch waits the rival mob for a good old-fashioned sumo shove-off. Biggest crowd wins the mela.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag to steer the whole mob
• Each gate row gives you ONE pick — do the math fast
• Red walls cost 30% — worth it or not?
• Final push-battle: both mobs shed blobs, the bigger sheds slower

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• That ×2-gate dopamine, 60 seconds at a time
• Blobs bounce away smiling — it's shoving, not violence
• One-thumb drag, fully offline vs AI
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the ×2-start ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. Tougher melas coming.
