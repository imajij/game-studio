## Short description (77/80)

## Full description
The gali is under attack and your shop is at the end of it. Runners, tanks-in-pot-helmets and swarms of little weirdos are marching the S-bend — and every fifth wave brings a crowned BOSS.

Your defense? Pure bazaar engineering. The Chai Cannon snipes. The Pakora Mortar splashes a whole crowd. The Fan Blower chills enemies to a crawl. Evolve each one to level 3 and watch it go golden.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a + spot to build; tap a tower to EVOLVE it
• Clear waves for ₹, leaks cost shop hearts (you have 5)
• Boss every 5th wave — bring splash damage
• Endless waves; your best wave is saved

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• 3 unit types × 3 levels = real build choices on 6 spots
• Creeps with helmets, crowns and attitude — all original
• One-thumb, fully offline, no account
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the free-evolve ad is always optional

Early access: a brand-new game from a solo developer. More units and maps coming.
