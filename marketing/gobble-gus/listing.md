## Full description
Gus is hungry. Gus is always hungry. Unfortunately, the sky is also raining anvils.

Drag Gus around the junkyard and catch the good stuff — cookies, tacos, melons, pie — while dodging anvils, bricks and spiky bois. Chain 12 catches in a row and Gus hits FEVER MODE: double points and zero chill.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag left and right to move Gus
• Catch snacks, dodge the heavy stuff
• Build a combo streak to trigger fever mode
• Three hits and Gus needs a nap

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Simple drag controls, frantic fun
• 8 original hand-drawn characters
• Fever mode chaos
• Works fully offline

Free to play, supported by family-safe ads. One optional ad wakes Gus up for another go.
