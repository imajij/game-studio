## Full description
Every 45 seconds the sky turns ugly. Your job: a cozy bunker that out-grows the weather. Dig new floors (every one earns supplies), move a little worker blob in, and above all — UPGRADE THAT HATCH. Ride the storm out and the bonus pours in; get caught under-hatched and the wind takes a bite of your stockpile.

HOW TO PLAY
• DIG new floors — each produces supplies per second
• HATCH upgrades keep you ahead of the storm level
• Survive a storm = big bonus + confetti; fail = lose 35%
• Supplies keep growing while you're away (4 h cap)

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• A 45-second pressure cycle that never stops escalating
• Watching your cutaway bunker go deeper and busier
• One-thumb, fully offline, no account
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the crate ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. More rooms, hazards and hatch tiers coming.
