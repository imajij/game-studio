## Full description
A mystery sound plays. Was that a Wet Slap… or an Alien Sneeze? An Angry Kettle… or a Robot Burp? Four names, a shrinking timer, and one rule: guess wrong or too slow and your little listener gets YEETED clean off the screen.

Every sound in the game is an original creation made just for this quiz — you've never heard them anywhere else, which is exactly why round one is harder than you think.

HOW TO PLAY
• Listen (tap 🔊 to replay)
• Pick one of four names before the bar runs out
• Timer starts at 6 seconds and tightens as your streak grows
• One wrong answer = yeet. Streak = score.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• 10 original sounds: Tiny Violin, Haunted Door, Disco Drip, Sad Horn…
• 20-second rounds, tick-tick-tick panic finishes
• One-thumb, fully offline, no account
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the second-chance ad is always optional

Early access: a brand-new game from a solo developer. More sounds and party rounds coming.
