## Full description
It's 3 AM. The lamp is ON. You are a moth. You know what to do.

Flutter Mothias ever upward through a sleepy bedroom sky — past ceiling fans, swiping cat paws and judgmental dust bunnies — chasing the one thing that matters: the lamp.

HOW TO PLAY
• Hold left or right to flutter that way (you never stop climbing)
• Dodge everything that spins, swipes or floats
• Grab sparkles for bonus points
• Climb as high as you can — the night speeds up

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Silky one-thumb endless climber
• Dreamy nighttime art with original characters
• Every run is different
• Works fully offline

Free to play, supported by family-safe ads. One optional ad revives you right where the paw got you.
