# Pin Panic — store listing

## App name
Pin Panic

## Short description (71/80)
Pull the right pin. The gold falls, the gunk follows. Don't mix them up.

## Full description
Gold balls above, purple gunk beside, four blue pins between them and the pot. Pull them in the right order and the gold pours home; pull wrong and the gunk beats it there. Real physics, zero mercy, instant retries.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a pin — it slides out with a clack
• Gravity handles the rest
• Enough gold in the pot = cleared; ONE gunk ball in = GUNKED
• Endless seeded vats, trickier tilts each level

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Proper tumbling ball physics in a cream wooden vat
• Ten-second "ohhh THAT pin" moments
• One-thumb taps, fully offline
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the skip ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. Lava vats coming.
