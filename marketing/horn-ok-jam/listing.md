## App name
Horn OK Jam

## Short description (76/80)
Every tap drives a vehicle straight ahead. Wrong order = HONK. Empty the lot.

## Full description
The bazaar parking lot has achieved perfect gridlock. Every vehicle will happily drive the way its windshield eyes face — straight into the next vehicle, unless you free its path first. Taps are limited. Honks are not.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a vehicle: clear path = vroom, gone; blocked = bump + honk + wasted tap
• Empty the lot before the taps run out
• Every jam is generated and verified solvable
• 6 → 12 vehicles as the jams get meaner

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Pure order-of-operations brain-tickle, 30 seconds a level
• Candy vehicles with faces, ring of tiny bazaar stalls
• One-thumb taps, fully offline
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the +3-taps ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. Trucks and tuk-tuk variants coming.
