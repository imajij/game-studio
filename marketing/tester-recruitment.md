## Quora — answer, not a promo post

Quora removes/collapses self-promotion. Answer a relevant question genuinely
(e.g. "How can I find 12 testers for Google Play closed testing?") and mention

**Answer draft:**

I'm going through this right now as a solo developer, so here's what actually works:

1. **Start with people you know.** Friends and family on Android are the most reliable testers because they stay opted in for the full 14 days, and that's the part that counts. Opt-ins that drop out early reset your progress.
2. **Use the tester-exchange communities.** Subreddits like r/AndroidClosedTesting and r/TestersCommunity exist for this — devs test each other's apps. Give back and people will test yours.
3. **Make joining effortless.** Use a Google Group or a simple form that collects Gmail addresses, then send each person the opt-in link plus a one-line reminder to stay installed for 14 days.
4. **Focus your testers.** If you have several apps, get 12 people on ONE app first. Spreading them thin means none of your apps reaches the bar.
5. **Ask testers to actually open the app** a few times. Google asks about tester engagement when you apply for production.
