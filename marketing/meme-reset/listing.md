## Full description
They reset the internet. All of it. The only way back is through — so grab the fedora paddle, launch the ball, and smash through wall after wall of neon bricks on a humming CRT, deeper and deeper into 2014.

Golden bricks drop ERA-CARDS: original tributes to the artifacts of the old internet — Shutter Shades, Planking Pete, Duckface Dana, the Loading Bar of Doom, the Dial-Up Demon. Collect all 10. Remember everything.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag to move the paddle, tap to launch
• Cyan bricks take two hits; golden bricks drop cards
• Clear the wall to go a level deeper (the ball speeds up)
• 3 balls per run — card collection is forever

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Crunchy retro CRT look with scanlines
• A collection screen's worth of "remember this?"
• One-thumb, fully offline, no account
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the extra-ball ad is always optional

Early access: a brand-new game from a solo developer. More eras coming.
