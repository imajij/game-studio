## Short description (66/80)

## Full description
A magenta cannon, a beach platform, and a fort of candy bricks that thinks it's permanent. Drag the dotted arc, release, and let momentum file the complaint. Topple three-quarters of the blocks off the platform and the fort is officially toda.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag to aim — trajectory dots show the arc
• Release to FIRE (the cannon does a little squash, as it should)
• 75% of blocks off the platform = cleared
• Stars for spare balls; forts grow taller every level

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Real physics clatter — every shot resolves differently
• It's blocks. Nothing lives in the fort. Pure guilt-free demolition
• One-thumb, fully offline
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the +3-balls ad is always optional

Early access from a solo developer. Moving forts coming.
