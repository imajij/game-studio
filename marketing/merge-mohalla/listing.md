## Full description
Drop desi street objects into the gali and watch physics do its thing. Two of a kind merge into the next, bigger thing: chai cups into samosas, samosas into jalebis, all the way up through pressure cookers, scooties and band speakers to… the Cursed Hybrid. What IS the final form? Find out.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag to aim, release to drop
• Matching objects merge into the next tier and shove everything around
• Keep the pile below the red line — overflow and it's GALI JAM
• Discover all 10 forms (your collection is saved)

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Proper tumbling physics — every drop reshuffles the pile
• "FOUND 7/10 THINGS" discovery log keeps pulling you back
• One-thumb, fully offline, no account
• Free to play with family-safe ads — the Jhadu Sweep ad is always optional

Early access: a brand-new game from a solo developer, still growing new objects.
