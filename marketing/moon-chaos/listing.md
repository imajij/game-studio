## Full description
Somebody has to clean the Moon, and that somebody is you. Sweep up lunar dust, haul it to the depot, load the shipment rocket and watch it blast off — then spend the cash on bigger brooms, a bigger pack, and bigger paydays.

HOW TO PLAY
• Hold anywhere to move — your sweeper cleans everything it walks over
• Carry the dust to the depot and DUMP it
• LAUNCH the rocket to turn dust into dollars
• Upgrade your tool, speed, pack and payouts
• Clean a zone to 100% spotless to unlock the next one

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Clean, cosy minimalist look — watch the grey Moon turn white, tile by tile
• One-thumb controls, satisfying sweep-and-ship loop
• Progress is saved on your device and earns while you're away
• Works fully offline — no account, no sign-up

Early access: this is a brand-new game still growing new zones, tools and automation. Made with love by a solo developer. Free to play, supported by family-safe ads — rewarded ads are always optional.
