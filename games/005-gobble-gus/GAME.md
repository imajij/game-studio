# GAME: Gobble Gus

- Status: prototype (greybox done; art + AdMob ids pending)
- Package: com.zintara.gobble_gus
- Target audience age: 13+ (general casual; not designed for children)
- Privacy policy URL: (required before release)

## Concept
Gus is a bottomless gremlin mouth. Drag him along the bottom to catch
falling snacks; dodge anvils, cacti and bricks. Chain catches to hit FEVER
(double points, rainbow Gus).

## Core loop (30–90 s, instant restart)
Catch (+1, +2 in fever) → combo builds → FEVER at 12 → hazards mixed in at
30% and falling ever faster → three bad bites and Gus taps out.

## Controls (one thumb, portrait)
Touch/drag anywhere — Gus follows your finger's X. Tap to restart.

## Cast (original)
Good: Cookie Carl · Taco Tina · Melon Mo · Pie Guy.
Bad (angular + red-eyed): Angry Anvil · Spiky Boi · Uncle Brick.

## Ad placements (wired, Google test ids until release)
- Rewarded revive (opt-in, once): full lives, sky cleared, keep score+fever
  progress reset.
- Interstitial: restart tap only, AdsManager caps.

## Art style (pending)
Junkyard-sunset orange palette, Flow sticker pass. Greybox: round faces for
food, angular grumps for hazards, Gus = big mouth with googly eyes.

## Sounds
Synth placeholders (chomp pitched up with combo, yuck buzz, fever arpeggio,
miss whistle, game-over walk, tap).
