## Concept
Power-wash ASMR ad genre: filthy 3D objects (auto-rickshaw → haathi statue →
water tanki, rotating) built from dozens of mud-caked panels; your thumb is the
jet. (Distinct from Moon Chaos: per-object aim-spray detailing, not a
walk-and-sweep tycoon.)

## Core loop (60–120 s per object, instant next)
Hold & drag → water jet tracks your thumb, panels melt from mud to vibrant
color with a sparkle-pop per finished panel → the object slowly turns on its
pedestal → 100% = CHAMAK GAYA confetti → next, dirtier thing.

## Controls (one thumb, portrait)
Hold and drag anywhere.

## Progression
Object number saved; three object types cycle.
