# GAME: Tile Toofan

- Status: in progress (batch 3)

- Target audience: Everyone incl. under-13 (declared child-directed, Families policy)
- Privacy policy URL: https://imajij.github.io/privacy.html

## Concept
Shortcut-run ad genre: hoover rainbow tiles onto your back-stack, every stretch
of sea silently spends one tile per plank under your feet, and leftovers get
cashed on a ×1→×5 multiplier runway.

## Core loop (40–80 s per toofan, instant retry)
Drag to steer → sweep tiles (stack pitch rises) → water gaps auto-lay planks
from your stack → dry stack over water = SPLASH → survive to the finish and
slide: remaining tiles = distance = multiplier zone = coins.

## Controls (one thumb, portrait)
Drag to steer; tap to start.

## Progression
Toofan level (longer course, wider gaps) + coin bank persist.

## Ad placements
- Rewarded (opt-in only): +15 golden tiles before the run.
- Interstitial: at result screens; AdsManager caps apply.
- Banner: none.

## Art style

×N Label3D zones, confetti landings.
