# GAME: Moth to the Lamp

- Status: prototype (greybox done; art + AdMob ids pending)
- Package: com.zintara.moth_to_the_lamp
- Target audience age: 13+ (general casual; not designed for children)
- Privacy policy URL: (required before release)

## Concept
Mothias, an unhinged fuzzy moth, climbs forever toward The Lamp. Hold the
left or right half of the screen to flutter that way; dodge ceiling fans,
cat paws and dust orbs; grab sparkles. One hit and you're squished.

## Core loop (30–90 s, instant restart)
Climb (score = meters + sparkles ×5) → speed and spawn rate ramp → near-miss
weaving → splat → "one more flight". Endless, no levels.

## Controls (one thumb, portrait)
Hold left/right half = flutter left/right. Release = drift. Tap to restart.

## Cast (original)
Mothias (hero moth) · Fan Bros (swaying ceiling fans, spinning blades) ·
Pawl (a cat paw stabbing in from the walls) · Dust Orbs (floating spiky
fuzz) · Sparkles (pickups). The Lamp is lore, never reached.

## Ad placements (wired, Google test ids until release)

  invincibility, keep your height.
- Interstitial: restart tap only, AdsManager caps.
