# GAME: Tempo Overload

- Status: in progress (batch 3)

- Target audience: Everyone incl. under-13 (declared child-directed, Families policy)
- Privacy policy URL: https://imajij.github.io/privacy.html

## Concept
Overloaded-cargo driving ad genre: a teal tempo, a candy box stack taller than
sense, a valley road full of potholes. Every swerve ripples up the stack with
growing looseness; boxes that sway past the limit tumble off behind you.

## Core loop (30–60 s per trip, instant retry)
Drag to steer → the stack sways (top boxes loosest) → potholes kick it →
deliver through the magenta arch: pay = surviving boxes × trip multiplier →
next trip = taller stack, meaner potholes. Lose every box = SAB GIR GAYA.

## Controls (one thumb, portrait)
Drag to steer; tap to start.

## Progression
Trip level (stack 5→∞, pothole density up) + coin bank persist.

## Ad placements
- Rewarded (opt-in only): CARGO STRAPS — double sway tolerance for the run.
