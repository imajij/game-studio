# GAME: Aura Farming Idle

- Status: in review (closed test)
- Package: com.zintara.aura_farming
- Target audience: Everyone incl. under-13 (declared child-directed, Families policy)
- Privacy policy URL: https://imajij.github.io/privacy.html

## Concept
Idle clicker where you farm "aura" by doing pointless cool things (adjusting your
collar, staring into the distance), buy ridiculous aura generators, and prestige
into RESPECT ranks.

## Core loop (endless sessions, idle pull-back)
Tap the guy → random cool pose + flavor text + aura → buy generators
(Sunglasses Rack → Monsoon Photoshoot) for passive aura/s → when aura piles up,
GAIN RESPECT: reset everything for a permanent ×0.25 multiplier per rank.
Aura keeps farming itself offline (4 h cap) — the comeback hook.

## Controls (one thumb, portrait)
Tap the character; tap shop buttons.

## Progression
6 generators with 1.16× cost growth, 6 respect ranks (Nobody → Aura Singularity),
offline earnings. Tap value scales with your rate so taps never feel dead.

## Ad placements
- Rewarded (opt-in only): 2× aura for 2 minutes.
- Interstitial: on prestige only (natural break); AdsManager frequency caps apply.
- Banner: none.

## Art style
Chill purple-night palette, vector hoodie-guy with sunglasses and 4 tap poses,
pulsing aura glow that grows with your rate, orbiting sparks (gold while boosted).
