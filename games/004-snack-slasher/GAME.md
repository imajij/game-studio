# GAME: Snack Slasher

- Status: prototype (greybox done; art + AdMob ids pending)
- Package: com.zintara.snack_slasher
- Target audience age: 13+ (general casual; not designed for children)
- Privacy policy URL: (required before release)

## Concept
Snacks with faces get lobbed across the screen; swipe through them to slice.
Sir Soap flies among them looking delicious and suspiciously clean — slice
him and the round ends instantly.

## Core loop (30–90 s, instant restart)
Waves lob 1–4 snacks → swipe arcs through several in one stroke (3+ in a
stroke = combo bonus equal to the combo) → waves speed up → drop 3 snacks or
slice Sir Soap → "one more". Score: 1 per slice + combo bonuses.

## Controls (one thumb, portrait)
Swipe to slice (white trail follows your finger). Tap to restart.

## Cast (original)
Pizza Pete · Donut Diane · Burrito Bandit · Sad Samosa · Chip Commander ·
**Sir Soap** (monocled soap bar, ~18% of spawns, do NOT slice).

## Ad placements (wired, Google test ids until release)
- Rewarded revive (opt-in, once): full lives, board cleared, keep score.
- Interstitial: restart tap only, AdsManager caps.

## Art style (pending)
Checkered-kitchen red/cream palette, Flow sticker pass. Greybox: faces on
colored circles; Sir Soap wears a monocle so he reads at a glance.

## Sounds
Synth placeholders (slice whoosh, splat pitched by size, combo chime, soap
alarm, miss whistle, game-over walk, tap).
