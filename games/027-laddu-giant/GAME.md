# GAME: Laddu Giant

- Status: in progress (batch 3)
- Package: com.zintara.laddu_giant
- Target audience: Everyone incl. under-13 (declared child-directed, Families policy)
- Privacy policy URL: https://imajij.github.io/privacy.html

## Concept
Giant-Rush size-runner: eat laddus to grow enormous, rocks and "fit ×1.2"
squeeze-gates cut you down, and the candy-brick wall at the end crumbles exactly
as deep as your final size. Growth fantasy with a payout.

## Core loop (35–70 s per gali, instant retry)
Drag to steer → laddus +0.1 size (camera pulls back as you swell) → rocks −0.25,
oversize at a gate trims you to fit in a burst of sparkles → the wall smash
converts size to coins brick by flying brick.

## Controls (one thumb, portrait)
Drag to steer; tap to start.

## Progression
Gali level (longer, rockier) + coin bank persist.
