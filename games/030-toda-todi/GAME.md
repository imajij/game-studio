# GAME: Toda Todi

- Status: in progress (batch 3)
- Package: com.zintara.toda_todi
- Target audience: Everyone incl. under-13 (declared child-directed, Families policy)
- Privacy policy URL: https://imajij.github.io/privacy.html

## Concept
Knock-Balls demolition ad genre: a magenta cannon, a candy-block fort on a
raised platform, five cannonballs. Drag the dotted arc, release, watch the
physics do comedy. Blocks only — nothing lives in the fort.

## Core loop (20–50 s per fort, instant next)
Aim with the trajectory dots → FIRE (cannon squashes, muzzle confetti) →
blocks clatter off the platform and pop → topple 75% to clear; stars = spare
balls. Forts are seeded towers with lintels, taller each level.

## Controls (one thumb, portrait)
Drag to aim, release to fire.

## Progression
Fort number saved; layers 3→6.

## Ad placements
- Rewarded (opt-in only): +3 balls after a fail, once per fort.
- Interstitial: at result screens; AdsManager caps apply.
- Banner: none.

## Art style
Beach-fort vibes on a Flow sand texture, teal platform on legs, candy bricks,
ink cannonballs, star chimes cascading on clears.

## Sound list
fire, hit, topple, star, win, tap — assets/audio/.
